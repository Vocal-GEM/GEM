## 2025-08-01 - Icon-only buttons lacking aria-labels\n**Learning:** In the Vocal GEM app, several icon-only buttons (like the mobile menu toggle in Sidebar or close button in QuickSettings) lack proper `aria-label` attributes, making them inaccessible to screen readers.\n**Action:** Add `aria-label` and `focus-visible` utility classes to icon-only buttons to improve accessibility and keyboard navigation.
